# Assessment Criteria

## Grade Breakdown

- Technical Implementation (50%): NLP pipeline design and justification, data exploration, evaluation protocol, final product quality, and creativity/complexity of your approach.
- Deliverable Quality (25%): Code quality and documentation, written report, and timeliness.
- Oral Presentation (15%): Content and structure, delivery, and depth demonstrated in the Q&A.
- Teamwork (10%): Balanced contribution across team members, assessed through a confidential peer evaluation survey (distributed after the presentation) and instructor observation (meeting attendance, commit history).

Note that the specific emphasis within each component may vary slightly depending on the nature of your project and what we have discussed in our meetings.
For example, a more research-oriented project may weight evaluation and methodology more heavily, while an engineering-focused project may place more emphasis on code quality and the final product.

## What a Strong Submission Looks Like

- A well-motivated pipeline where each design choice is justified, not just copied from tutorials.
- Thorough data exploration that informs your modeling decisions.
- Multiple appropriate evaluation metrics with a baseline and some error analysis.
- A report that structures the work clearly and is honest about its limitations.
- Clean, reproducible code with a working README.
- A presentation that highlights your key contributions and responds confidently to questions.
- Evidence that every team member contributed meaningfully.

## Suggestions for the Final Days

- Tighten the report's discussion and limitations sections.
- Polish your README, requirements, and reproduction instructions.
- Add a baseline comparison or brief error analysis if you have not already.
- Rehearse the presentation with timing in mind.
